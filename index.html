Alumni Nexus

The future of alumni engagement. An intelligent platform to connect, engage, and empower institutional networks.

Team: BridgED | Institution: Noida Institute Of Engineering and Technology

Domain: Smart Education

Problem Statement

Educational institutions struggle with scattered, outdated alumni data, leading to a critical 'engagement gap'. This disconnect prevents current students from accessing valuable mentorship, limits placement opportunities through referrals, and hinders institutions from leveraging their most powerful asset—their alumni network. Our project solves this by creating a centralized, intelligent, and self-updating platform that transforms a passive database into a vibrant, engaged community.

Objectives

  • To create a single, centralized, and secure alumni network.
  • To enable students to easily access verified alumni for mentorship and career guidance.
  • To improve placement outcomes by leveraging a powerful alumni referral network.
  • To provide institutions with actionable analytics on alumni career progression.

Proposed Solution

  • AI-powered alumni management → Automates data collection & updates.
  • Community engagement → Drives active participation beyond static databases.
  • Ethical data enrichment → Keeps profiles updated responsibly.
  • Gamification features → Encourage alumni to update and stay connected.
  • Structured mentorship & referral system → Direct support for students.
  • Intelligence Engine → Actively maintains data freshness and extracts actionable insights.
